# What's new

## 0.1.52
_2026-10-21_
- Fixed: A plain 1909 Lincoln cent (and other scarce but common-enough coins) is no longer sometimes flagged as a possible replica.

## 0.1.49
_2026-10-20_
- Fixed: A long message to the maker is no longer cut off silently; the box now counts the characters and Send waits until the message is short enough.

## 0.1.48
_2026-10-20_
- New: You can now hold a button and talk to the tutor or to the maker instead of typing.

## 0.1.47
_2026-10-19_
- New: You can add a coin straight from a coin folder: tap Add a coin, photograph the front and back, and it goes into that folder.

## 0.1.45
_2026-10-18_
- New: Changed: Folders are now called coin folders everywhere in the Collection.

## 0.1.44
_2026-10-18_
- New: A folder can be renamed: open it and tap Rename.

## 0.1.43
_2026-10-18_
- Fixed: Tapping Update now always finishes by reloading the app, instead of sometimes sitting on "Updating…".

## 0.1.42
_2026-10-17_
- Fixed: The Collection gives the coins more room: the Wish list sits on the folder shelf, Looking at is one row, and the camera button no longer covers a coin.

## 0.1.41
_2026-10-17_
- New: In your Collection, tap Select (or press and hold a coin) to choose coins and remove them, with Undo for five seconds.

## 0.1.40
_2026-10-17_
- New: Folders in the Collection, like a coin album: put coins in a folder and see their worth together as one rough range.

## 0.1.39
_2026-10-16_
- New: The photo reader now tells a replica or copy from the real coin, names it "Replica", and gives it a replica's worth, not the real coin's.

## 0.1.37
_2026-10-13_
- New: The smallest countries, such as Singapore, the Vatican, Monaco and Malta, are on the globe now: you can search for them and tap them.

## 0.1.36
_2026-10-13_
- New: On the Globe you can pinch to zoom, and tapping next to a small country now opens it, with a short list to choose from when several are close together.

## 0.1.33
_2026-10-13_
- New: Ask the tutor "How rare is it?" on a coin and it answers from the real number struck, saying how many survive is not known, and never a price.

## 0.1.32
_2026-10-12_
- New: Coins and notes Legend has no table for can now be asked how rare they are, and the answer is marked as the tutor's estimate.

## 0.1.31
_2026-10-12_
- New: A coin's page now says how rare it is by how many the Mint made, with a strip showing where its year sits, and Special years shows the number struck.

## 0.1.30
_2026-10-12_
- New: Other pieces such as tokens can now be shared as a card, and they light up their country on the Globe.

## 0.1.29
_2026-10-12_
- New: Tokens, medals and other pieces now get a rough guess of what they might be worth, and the tutor can tell you about them.

## 0.1.27
_2026-10-12_
- New: Add now has an Other choice for tokens, medals and other collectibles, and the photo reader names them.

## 0.1.26
_2026-10-12_
- New: You can now keep other pieces such as tokens and medals in your Collection, with a name like "Subway token", and find them again with a new Other filter and search.

## 0.1.24
_2026-10-10_
- New: The key on Unlock shows zeros with a slash, so a 0 is never read as an o.

## 0.1.23
_2026-10-10_
- Fixed: Add a pile of coins fast: photograph front and back, Name it, and go straight on; each fills itself in as Legend names it.

## 0.1.21
_2026-10-10_
- New: Unlock shows this phone's key as a QR code for Dad's Postern to scan.

## 0.1.19
_2026-10-10_
- New: Wish list: keep a coin you saw at the store with its price, save up for it, and move it into your collection with 'Got it!'.

## 0.1.17
_2026-10-10_
- Fixed: Unlock answers in a few seconds, not tens of seconds, on a phone that has no licence yet.

## 0.1.16
_2026-10-10_
- New: Ask the tutor about any of your coins or a country, and it answers from that coin; it can suggest a setting, which changes only if you tap Apply.

## 0.1.15
_2026-10-10_
- New: Legend now gives each coin a rough worth, clearly marked as an estimate, and shows a rough worth for your whole collection and, in the store, for a coin you are only looking at.

## 0.1.14
_2026-10-10_
- New: Photograph a coin or a banknote front and back and Legend names it for you to check, or just look at coins without adding them.

## 0.1.13
_2026-10-10_
- New: You can tell the maker what you would like added or what is wrong, from the small "…" on each screen or from Settings, and the Globe now shows a one-time tip.

## 0.1.12
_2026-10-10_
- New: Learn now teaches grading, mint marks and special years using the coins you have, and Your path shows what you have learned and what to try next.

## 0.1.11
_2026-10-10_
- New: You can now share a picture of your globe or a card of one coin or note from the Share button, and only that picture leaves the phone.

## 0.1.10
_2026-10-10_
- New: Legend now opens on an Unlock screen until Dad has issued this phone a licence.

## 0.1.9
_2026-10-10_
- New: The Collection now shows everything you've collected as a photo grid or list, with search, filters for coins, bills and countries, and a page for each item with its photos, facts, notes, edit and remove.

## 0.1.8
_2026-10-10_
- New: The Quarters screen shows a map of the United States lit with the quarters you have, a checklist of every quarter, and what you are still missing for each state.

## 0.1.7
_2026-10-10_
- New: The Globe: turn it with a finger to see the countries your coins come from lit in gold, tap a country to see your coins from there and the money it uses now and used before, and see which countries you have nothing from yet.

## 0.1.1
_2026-10-10_
- New: Legend now has a bottom bar with Collection, Globe, Quarters, Learn and Settings, and a Settings screen where you can change the theme, the text size and more.

## 0.1.0
_2026-10-10_
