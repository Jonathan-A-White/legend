## Vision (draft, from his words above)
- A coin and banknote collection app: photograph an item, the factory names it, it joins his collection.
- For Paul, who is eight, and any serious collector; grown-up, never kiddy.
- It teaches from his own coins: grading, mints, special years, geography, rough worth.
- Most important: he grows in skill and curiosity, and the app is elegant to use.
- Lines: private unless he chooses to share; nothing that feeds greed (worth is a rough range, never a price).

## How feedback is weighed
Feedback that fits this vision is approved and built elegantly; where people differ, a setting; anything crossing a line or changing the vision goes to the Governor.
